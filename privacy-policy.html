Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This policy explains how The Social Aura ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit thesocialaura.ca, contact us, or use our services. We are a digital marketing agency based in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. This includes your name, email address, phone number, business name or website, and the message you send us through our contact form, email, WhatsApp or social media messages.

Information collected automatically. If you accept cookies, we and our service providers collect information about how you use our website, such as pages viewed, links clicked, device and browser type, approximate location, the site that referred you, and your IP address.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and provide free audits, quotes and services.
  • To understand how our website is used and to improve it.
  • To measure and improve our advertising on platforms such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

With your permission, our website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and the Meta Pixel to measure and improve our ads. These tools use cookies and similar technologies. They only load after you click Accept on our cookie notice. If you click Decline, they do not load.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of our home page, or by clearing cookies in your browser. You can also manage ad preferences in your Google and Meta account settings.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed. These include our website host (GitHub), email and document tools (Google Workspace), analytics (Google), advertising and messaging platforms (Meta, including Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp), and Formspree, the service that delivers your contact form messages to us. We may also share information when required by law.

5. Storage outside Canada

Some of these providers store or process information in other countries, including the United States. Those countries may have different privacy laws from Canada.

6. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or to meet legal requirements, and then delete or anonymize it.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can withdraw your consent to our use of it, subject to legal limits. Email us at info@thesocialaura.ca and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Our website and services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
